TIDAL GEOMETRIES

A sibling series to Seeded Geometries. Two ring centers per piece, every ring welded exactly to the square grid — one fused lattice that breathes. Warm amber, coral, and ember washes glow on absolute black, with 99 neon accent dots blinking around the rings. Every 8 seconds, a double wave rolls through and the whole piece spins, stretches, and settles back to rest. Each token is grown from its own unique seed.
