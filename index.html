Tidal Geometries

A sibling series to Seeded Geometries: concentric ring lattices welded exactly to the square grid. Each piece is rendered from a single 64-character token hash: fixed slices of the hash choose the coarse traits (palette, density, dot grid, wave trains), while domain-separated sub-seeds feed independent random streams for all micro-detail. Same hash → same piece, forever, anywhere. No two mints share a seed.

Two ring centers per piece; every ring/spoke/grid joint is a single fused vertex. Stippled bone linework on absolute black, washed in the token’s hue, with 99 neon accent dots on the cool bioluminescent spectrum. The 8-second loop runs 2 seconds of rest, a 4-second expanding-ring wave, and 2 seconds of rest.

Demo hashes are deterministic stand-ins. A production deployment derives the token hash from the mint blockhash instead. Cards below are still frames; the motion loop above is the live system.
